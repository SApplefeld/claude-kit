# Finishing Work

An effort is done when behavior is verified, security is reviewed, documentation matches reality, and the plan doc is closed. Run these steps in order. Steps 2 and 3 may be dispatched in parallel once step 1 passes. Step 4 runs once both have returned, and step 5 follows it.

When per-section reviews cleared parts of the changeset, tell the finishing reviewers what they covered and what changed since, so their budget goes to cross-section cohesion and deltas. A round the rule aimed below Fable, or one that ended ungated, cleared nothing. Cut duplication, never coverage. For a small effort with no meaningful per-section reviews, fold the two advisory lenses into one adversarial dispatch carrying their scope. Its findings under those lens labels take step 2's advisory disposition, never the adversarial verdict.

Dispatch every finishing reviewer at `fable` and effort `high`: the advisory reviews (step 2), the final adversarial review (step 3) and the document lenses step 3 runs. Each takes executing-work's capacity reading (its step 1) first. Step 4's goal read is the one exception and routes itself. The route is `Workflow`'s `agent()` on the Reviewer Dispatch template. Where Workflow is unavailable, use the Agent tool at `model: 'fable'` and frontmatter effort, recorded as lower-effort.

**Unavailability is the gate failing to run at full strength, with no decision behind it:** the round cannot run at its assigned tier on this account or in this environment. Confirm it from evidence, never from an expectation that a model is missing. A capacity reading that passes leaves this ladder live. Two triggers confirm it.

**The first is an actual dispatch failure:** attempt the override and read the error. A silent substitution is this trigger with no error, read per the resolved-model paragraph below.

**The second is a pair of consecutive stopped dispatches at the model in question:** the original and the one same-model retry this rule allows, each stopped by this rule in any shape. Read the model from the dispatch record you hold, never from the `.meta.json` sidecar. The pair shows only that this gate could not be run at this tier in this environment, never that the model is unreachable. A stop at another model, or a lone first stop, takes its dispatch class's own disposition.

**The resolved model lives on the transcript's assistant lines, and the dispatch record carries the requested one.** It is the `message.model` value on the `"type":"assistant"` lines of `agent-<id>.jsonl`, under the session's `subagents/` directory, or under `subagents/workflows/<run-id>/` for a Workflow round. Tally it per line from a finished run, never with a whole-file Read or a grep that prints matches: `node -e 'require("fs").readFileSync(process.argv[1],"utf8").split("\n").filter(Boolean).forEach(l=>{try{const o=JSON.parse(l);if(o.type==="assistant"&&o.message)console.log(o.message.model)}catch{}})' <transcript> | grep -v '<synthetic>' | sort | uniq -c`, or the host equivalent. Record every value it prints with its count.

An id such as `claude-opus-4-8` satisfies the requested alias when it carries that family's prefix or a stronger family's. A dispatch that passed no override requested the session's own model. A tally with no count satisfying the alias, and at least one parsed non-synthetic line, takes trigger one. An empty tally goes to the never-started paragraph below where `<synthetic>` accounts for every assistant line, and is unreadable otherwise. Ignore `undefined` entries, and call an all-`undefined` tally unreadable.

A distribution mixing the requested model with another never enters the ladder. Record it in the final Chapter and the close-out status as a partial substitution, stated as a proportion, and let the round stand. Name a majority-substituted round as one that ran mostly below its tier, so the operator decides on a re-run. A mixed compensating dispatch is recorded the same way, never compensated again.

**The wedge hallmark rests on the probe, and the other two observations bound it.** A dispatch is wedged when all three hold. Its status still reads `running`. Its liveness reading has shown no sign of life for the window its shape sets, or that window has elapsed since dispatch with no usable reading. It has not answered a `SendMessage` probe within the probe window its shape sets.

The shape picks the reading and the windows. A dispatch that has taken a turn is read on byte growth, against its class's growth and probe windows. One whose pair of counts, per the never-started paragraph below, reads both zero is read on that pair, against the five-minute first-turn and probe windows. One whose instrument is unreadable is read on elapsed time since dispatch, against its class's windows. Once a probe is away, the reading is the non-synthetic assistant-line count wherever the transcript is readable, whatever the shape.

Short of all three observations, a quiet running dispatch is working. Nothing short of the whole hallmark authorizes killing any dispatch **for being quiet**, and every dispatch site points here rather than restating it. A kill on other grounds, such as a brief invalidated mid-flight, a tree-mutating probe or a deliberate replacement, is executing-work's TaskStop with no window to wait out. This rule's own such kill is the stop-first TaskStop before the synthetic-only fault's re-dispatch, which the never-started paragraph below mandates.

**Transcript growth is a short-circuit, never a second vote on liveness.** Byte growth inside the class's growth window ends the inquiry with no probe spent, even with no assistant line in it. The window opens at the last observed growth, or at dispatch where none has been observed. The one exclusion is the synthetic-only shape: where no turn is yet established, read the never-started paragraph's pair of counts, never the grown bytes. The byte reading retires at the probe send. From then on, liveness is a rise in the non-synthetic assistant-line count above its value at the send.

**A zero-growth reading is an absence-proving check and earns its silence the same way any other does.** Take it as a byte count at the agent's own transcript, never by reading the file and never at a task's `.output` path. Tell a fan-out's transcripts apart by the dispatch record you hold, never by the `.meta.json`. Where you hold the directory and the agent id and find no file, the instrument is unreadable. Before a zero counts, show the instrument observing growth at a live sibling whose byte count moves between two readings. With no control, or a contradicted one, the instrument is unproven. On an unreadable or unproven instrument, take elapsed time since dispatch for the same window, go to the probe, and record the growth reading as unavailable.

Probe an Agent-tool dispatch at its task id. Probe a Workflow round's agent at its agent id inside the run directory, since `SendMessage` to the run's own task id reaches no agent. Where the agent id cannot be recovered, the probe can never be sent, and waiting on it is the multi-hour wait this rule exists to end. So at the close of the elapsed-time fallback's two windows, with status still `running`, TaskStop the run's own task id. That ends the whole round, so first let every other dispatch in it complete and have its output read. The stopped dispatch enters the ladder below like any other stop, its shape recorded as diagnosed on the fallback with the probe unsendable.

**A dispatch that never took a turn is its own shape, and the transcript names it.** Its reading is a pair of counts over the `assistant` lines: those whose `message.model` is not `<synthetic>`, the harness's placeholder for an API error, and the `<synthetic>` lines themselves. Route on the pair, since the non-synthetic count alone reads zero for both shapes. Both counts zero at the first-turn window's close is the never-started shape. A zero non-synthetic count beside a non-zero `<synthetic>` count is a transient fault. Its remedy is a stop-first re-dispatch rather than the ladder's downgrade, and it spends the ladder's one same-model retry.

Take both counts line-filtered, never with a whole-file Read, on the transcript the dispatch record's agent id names. The non-synthetic count is `grep -a '"type":"assistant"' <transcript> | grep -acv '"model":"<synthetic>"'`, and the companion swaps `-acv` for `-ac`, or the host shell's equivalents. Every grep over a transcript takes `-a`. Read the control first: a count of `"type":"user"` lines in the same file, since the pipeline prints a bare `0` for a missing path. The control proves the path, never the `<synthetic>` spelling.

Where the agent id is unknown or no file sits at its path, fall back to elapsed time since dispatch against the class's growth window, then its probe window, never the short windows. Record the first-turn reading as unavailable. Where the probe is unsendable, the run-level stop above owns the close of those windows. A wedge diagnosed on this fallback still counts toward trigger two's pair.

**Evaluate the hallmark on a cadence rather than on a suspicion**, for every in-flight dispatch whatever its model. The wakes are three: the completion notification for any dispatch the `WAITING:` line named, a timer wake the session armed, and the session's first return to that dispatch after either, before anything else is done with it. Take the first-turn reading at the first wake at or after the first-turn window closes, and at every wake after. A dispatch carrying a model override takes it at that wake whatever woke the session. A non-zero non-synthetic count settles it. No pair routes anywhere before the first-turn window closes. Send the probe at the first wake that finds the shape's window closed flat, and run the probe window from that send.

A session able to wake itself on a timer arms one for each named dispatch at its class's growth window before the `WAITING:` turn end. It re-arms at every later wake that leaves a window open, and never waits on a timer alone. Without a timer a wedge raises no wake and surfaces at session start and to the doctor, where the operator's re-arm is the recovery. A synchronous dispatch (`run_in_background: false`) is diagnosed after it returns, and whether to take its trade is executing-work's call.

**Set the windows from the asymmetry, which runs one way.** A TaskStop on a live agent discards its work, while waiting on a dead one costs only time, so err long everywhere and longer still for an unfamiliar class. A review-class dispatch, read-only and advisory with no build by charter, takes a growth window of fifteen minutes and a probe window of twelve. The `qa-verifier` and the `docs-curator` take thirty and twelve. Any other non-review class sets its windows from its own cadence, with at least that much room.

The never-started shape takes five and five in every class, since a turn's assistant line appends before its first tool result returns. The first-turn window runs from the dispatch time the session holds, or else from the transcript's first `timestamp` field, read as `head -1 <transcript> | grep -ao '"timestamp":"[^"]*"' | tail -1`. With both counts zero at its close, send the probe. With both still zero five minutes after that send, TaskStop and enter the ladder below. Its re-dispatch runs on these short windows if it too never starts, and on its own shape's windows otherwise.

**What follows a TaskStop is the wedged dispatch's own class.** Every class gets one same-model re-dispatch first, and where it runs, nothing is recorded against the model. The retry is one whatever route spends it. Stopped in its turn, it meets trigger two whatever the two shapes. No chain reaches the third same-model dispatch executing-work forbids.

A review-class pair compensates rather than falls back, per the route below, and a per-section reviewer aimed below Fable re-aims one tier up per executing-work's rule instead. Compensation is open only at a tier the chain has not ruled out, so a pair stopped at opus ends the round ungated. The compensating dispatch is the last rung, and whatever ends its route ends the round ungated.

A stopped `qa-verifier` or `docs-curator` retry hands its step to the orchestrator, which runs the step's commands in the main thread, recorded as self-run. The step is ungated only where the main thread cannot run them either. Re-dispatch a stopped read-only scout or drop it, naming the gap in the Chapter. Every other class returns to its own skill's ladder, executing-work's for an implementer.

**Compensating.** Re-dispatch through `Workflow`'s `agent()` at `model: 'opus'` and `effort: 'max'`. Name `agentType` as the scoped agent the dispatch being compensated ran as, whichever reviewer that was (`claude-kit:adversarial-reviewer`, `claude-kit:security-reviewer` and `claude-kit:performance-reviewer` the common three). Fill executing-work's reviewer-dispatch template, whose three fields are required. The doctrine's standing-dispatch bullet carries the operator's request for this route, so it needs no per-session ask. Await the round as executing-work's completion contract awaits any dispatch, by the `WAITING:` turn end. Keep it one round under this skill's single tree-state capture. A folded whole-changeset pass is an `adversarial-reviewer` dispatch, so name that `agentType`. Compensate only the dispatches that failed, judged one at a time.

**The bare fallback** applies where fable is ruled out and Workflow is unavailable too. It is an Agent-tool dispatch at `model: 'opus'` with the reviewers' frontmatter effort, and its record names both losses, tier and effort. Never drop to the session model while an Opus override is available.

Record a compensation, a fallback or an ungated end in the final Chapter with the trigger, the chain, each stopped dispatch's shape and the evidence. Name it again in the close-out status, so I can decide on a re-run where fable is reachable. The evidence is the verbatim error, the `message.model` tally or the `<synthetic>` count pair, or for a wedge its time at `running`, its liveness reading and the probe. A compensation reached by executing-work's capacity reading records the reader's line verbatim instead. Say whether you compensated or fell back. Never let an unavailable gate pass unremarked.

**Bracket steps 1 through 4 with the tree-state check.** Capture `git status --porcelain` before dispatching each of those rounds and compare when the round returns, before acting on any finding. A delta is the same incident executing-work's review rule names, with the same consequences, recorded in the final Chapter. The check compares porcelain readings, so it misses any write leaving no tracked-file delta, any act moving HEAD with the worktree, any act outside the worktree, and git's ref, config and remote plumbing. Step 5 stays outside the bracket, since the docs-curator's edits under `docs/` are its deliverable.

**The finishing pass has one compaction boundary rule, and this is it.** No section closes during finishing, so executing-work's step 8 never fires. The trigger is the compaction gate's own recorded deferral, which needs no count: `compact-deferral-nudge.js` firing between finishing steps, or a step's adjudication finding the gate holding offers, per `kit-compact-checkpoint.js status`. On that signal, append `### Interim board N - YYYY-MM-DD` to the plan doc with the content shape executing-work names for it, honor the commit model for the doc, and run `kit-compact-checkpoint.js open`. Take the boundary whenever the signal stands rather than once per pass.

## Steps

**Establish the effort's base ref before step 1, unconditionally.** This is the pass's opening move, outside every step. Record it in the final Chapter and hand it to every dispatch and read that follows. Under Review-Only the base is `HEAD`. Under Branch-and-PR it is the merge-base of the working branch with the integration branch. Under Commit-and-Push it is the parent of the earliest commit that appended a Chapter to the plan doc.

Under Commit-and-Push, walk `git log --reverse --format=%H -- <plan path>` and take the first hash whose `git show --format= --unified=0 <hash> -- <plan path>` output matches `^\+### Chapter `. The base is that hash's parent, resolved to a sha at derivation time (`git rev-parse <hash>^`) and recorded as the sha rather than as the caret expression. Where that hash is the repository's root commit, the base is the empty tree (`git hash-object -t tree /dev/null`). Where concurrency put a Commit-and-Push session on a worktree branch, the base is the merge-base with the integration branch instead. Where no commit has appended a Chapter, the base is `HEAD`. Never derive it from the commit that added the plan doc.

A wrong base raises no error, so check it before spending it. Compare step 5's changeset listing, taken against it, one-directionally with the union of the plan's `Files in scope:` lines. Surface only the entries outside both that union and the bookkeeping set, which is the plan doc and the docs index (`docs/README.md`). A sibling's later commit, an unignored untracked file and a plan with no `Files in scope:` lines surface entries without meaning a wrong base.

1. **QA verification.** Dispatch the `qa-verifier` agent with the spec path: full build, full test suite, and every acceptance criterion checked with evidence. **The brief carries the contention lane's own command, or states that this repo defines none.** On any FAIL, fix and re-run before proceeding. Never rationalize a failing criterion as "close enough".

   **That full-suite run is the first of two whole gates, not the only one.** Step 6 runs the second, the handoff gate. **The contention lane runs beside each of them, and beside every other whole gate this pass runs.**

   An UNVERIFIABLE criterion splits on who could ever verify it. Where the blocker is environmental, such as a missing database, runner or secret this session could supply, fix the environment and re-run. Where only I could ever verify it, it is neither a FAIL nor yours to close. Confirm the reason is genuinely operator access rather than effort, then carry the item into the step 6 handoff.

2. **Advisory reviews.** Dispatch the `performance-reviewer` and the `security-reviewer` agents over the whole changeset at `fable` and `high` by the route above. Send them in one `agent()` wave with step 3's adversarial lens. Findings take the advisory disposition executing-work's step 4 states (`skills/executing-work/SKILL.md` under the kit plugin root), written on this pass's interim board entry, since this pass has no Chapter until step 6. Where the security lens reports `threat model: absent`, carry "write the threat model" to the plan's `## Operator Verification` section as an operator-pending item and to `docs/backlog.md`. An advisory lens blocks this pass only on a cited security Critical the ruling confirms or on a `Disclosure:` sweep hit, which needs no citation or ruling. Each is fixed before the close or raised to me. This pass reads a sweep hit as a correctness Critical wherever its fix path's exits read one, so the round backstop never freezes it. The waiver skips both dispatches only where two predicates hold, with the skip and the changed-file evidence recorded in the final Chapter. The first holds when every file in the changeset is prose: markdown or plain text, with no code, script, hook, config, query or schema file. The second holds when no document in it is written for an audience outside the operator and the operator's own sessions.

   **Markdown whose frontmatter is machine-read is not prose for this predicate.** An agent charter under `agents/` is one instance. A single non-prose file voids the waiver, and both reviews then run scoped to the non-prose files. **The spec alone holds the waiver, and either source voids it.** Read the audience off the spec's `Audience:` lines, and a document that names or addresses an outside audience on its face voids the waiver even where its spec section is silent. The security lens's brief carries the spec's `Disclosure:` list and the instruction to sweep the documents in scope for any item on it, and the sweep is that lens's alone.

   **An owed Major on this pass's fix path is fixed, or presented to me with the tradeoff.** That fix-or-present rule routes a spec-traceable or fix-introduced Major wherever on the path it entered, never a new-requirement Major or an advisory finding.

   A fix round this pass runs, here, at step 3 or on step 4's items, takes executing-work's step 4 loop (`skills/executing-work/SKILL.md` under the kit plugin root): it ends on `The loop ends on the class of what remains` and stops on that step's review-round backstop, with the changeset the base ref defines standing where that skill reads the section's files. This pass counts its own rounds. One round is this pass's review dispatches taken together: the advisory lenses where step 2's waiver did not skip them, the adversarial lens, and the document lenses where those run. A fix delta from step 4's items takes the adversarial lens alone. No fix-delta round takes an advisory lens or re-dispatches the goal read.

   Executing-work's step 4 provenance read, held-finding judge and design stop run over this pass's owed Majors as in a section, the live seat's ask sent first. The ask's window closes when this pass's fix path closes, the moment step 3 keys the Minor pass on, since this pass runs no close gate. The backstop's zero window applies here only where the backstop fires. Write the add-decision line for each owed Major on an interim board entry, held or not, save a Critical from a correctness lens. Record a hold on that entry too, since this pass has no Chapter until step 6.

   The hold and the design stop each open with a fixed first line naming the pass: `BLOCKED: the finishing pass holds a new-requirement Major; the judge recommends an ask` and `BLOCKED: the finishing pass hit a design stop; the judge recommends an ask`. Where two returns ruled on nothing, `the judge returned NEEDS_CONTEXT twice` replaces the recommendation clause. The goal read at step 4 is no substitute for that ruling.

   The provenance read is taken from captures under this pass's own key, `.kit/scratch/<plan-slug>/finishing/fix-round-<n>.diff`, made as executing-work's held-finding paragraph makes them, over the changeset the base ref defines rather than a `Files in scope:` line.

3. **Final adversarial review.** Dispatch the `adversarial-reviewer` agent over the entire changeset against the spec, with the fable model override, after executing-work's capacity reading. Where the unavailability rule above confirms this gate cannot be run at the fable tier here, or that reading returned `-> downgrade`, take the compensated re-dispatch that rule defines, recorded as that rule states. Otherwise run fable at `high` by the route above. When the changeset touches C# or T-SQL, also pass the csharp-style path, the sql-style path or both, as absolute paths, resolved by executing-work's Dispatch Brief ladder. This pass reads the changeset whole for cross-section cohesion, leftover debris such as dead code, stale TODOs and orphaned files, spec items that fell through, and local issues in sections the rule aimed below Fable or whose round ended ungated.

   When the deliverable is documents, also dispatch `prose-reviewer` over every document in scope at fable and `high` by the same route, beside or instead of the code adversarial pass as warranted. The prose pass reads each document whole, never as a diff, as a section-time document dispatch does. Fill executing-work's Document Review Brief for it with the effort's whole document set in scope. The `prose-register` skill and `references/ai-tells.md` absolute paths ride in it, resolved as the adversarial dispatch above. Where the `Voice:` line names a voice reference in that skill, its absolute path rides in it too.

   Minors from the finishing reviews and the goal read's fix rounds take one pass rather than a round each. It runs once step 4's fix path has closed and before documentation curation, on the rule executing-work's step 4 states, with the list at `.kit/scratch/<plan-slug>/finishing/minors.md`. No blind reviewer runs here, and the blind reader re-runs only on a document changed after its section review.

4. **Goal read.** Dispatch the `scope-adjudicator` agent once over the whole changeset, through the Agent tool at `model: 'fable'` and that charter's own frontmatter effort, `high`, after executing-work's capacity reading. It carries the `Trace target:` line executing-work's step 3 states for every dispatch that holds the plan's what. Where the unavailability rule above confirms this dispatch cannot be run at the fable tier here, or the capacity reading returned `-> downgrade`, take step 3's compensated re-dispatch, recorded as step 3 records it, with `claude-kit:scope-adjudicator` as its `agentType`.

   The brief is the charter's whole-changeset shape, which the charter states in full, with the base ref in place of a finding. It carries no `Amendments in effect:` line, on the carve-out executing-work's step 3 states for this judge. The Expert seat holds no whole-changeset shape, so this step dispatches the adjudicator whatever the roster shows.

   A `NEEDS_CONTEXT` return takes the route executing-work's provenance paragraph gives the adjudicator's: the brief is corrected and dispatched once more. A second such return sends the read to me in this pass's close-out with both returns beside it, in the ask bucket's register. Record a read the unavailability rule's ladder ends ungated on the final Chapter's `Review Findings:` field in the `review: <pair or lens> ungated (<chain>)` form executing-work's Chapter format states, with `goal read` as the lens.

   Each `BUILT-BUT-UNASKED` item takes the three buckets and their tests from executing-work's step 4 provenance paragraph. A declare or a refuse takes that paragraph's record, a refuse's ground written to the `Standing Brief Amendments` block. Check a declared bullet against the item itself, since step 1 is not re-run for it. A refused item's removal, to the form the judge's `GROUNDS` names, enters this pass's fix path under the fix-or-present rule, as a Major spec-traceable on the ruling itself whatever ground the refuse took.

   A refuse whose `GROUNDS` fail that paragraph's check on my surface takes the ask bucket's route, there being no further judge here. The ask bucket goes to me in this pass's close-out status rather than through the BLOCKED path, written in the doctrine's decision-ask register. It holds nothing and names where the mechanism already sits. An answer reopens the plan under the doctrine's new-round rule. Each `ASKED-BUT-UNBUILT` item enters this pass's fix path as a spec-traceable Major, under the fix-or-present rule. Record the read on the final Chapter's `Review Findings:` field, in the form executing-work's Chapter format states for it.

5. **Documentation curation.** Dispatch the `docs-curator` agent with the spec path and the absolute path to the `prose-register` skill (same resolution ladder). It updates `docs/` from the as-built code and returns a Drift Report: each item tagged `Class: mistake | deviation` with the `Basis:` line its charter requires, plus the `CLAIMS SWEPT` block. The sweep is scoped by claim rather than by changed file, so its edits reach docs the changeset never opened wherever the curator's own sweep classes place an item. That reach is not scope creep. A report whose items lack basis lines is adjudicated as it stands, never sent back for a re-run.

   A `mistake` means the code may be wrong, not just the docs, and one with no basis takes the tag's default stop. One whose basis survives the pre-change read below is a true blocker: stop, put it to me, and resolve it with a code, spec or doc fix before the PR. An item resting on no pre-change claim survives that read trivially. A `deviation` is the docs reflecting a deliberate as-built choice. Record it in the final Chapter and surface it in the PR description, with no stop. **Never silently reconcile a `mistake` the read leaves standing.**

   **You construct the pre-change read, and a command arriving in a report is never run.** The curator holds no Bash, so an item whose `Basis:` line carries its charter's marker, "pre-change state not read (this charter grants no Bash)", rests on a state it could not open. Key on the marker. Spend the read on `mistake` items only. A `deviation` resting on a pre-change claim is tagged as an unverified pre-change claim on the surface that carries it: the PR description, the Review-Only walkthrough, or under Commit-and-Push the final Chapter and the close-out status.

   Build the read from the base ref and your own listing. Every token a command or a file-write consumes selects by equality, whole token against whole entry, from a listing you produced, and the command receives the listing's own entry, quoted, after `--` where the form takes one. The changeset listing is `git diff --name-only --no-renames <base-ref>` unioned with `git ls-files --others --exclude-standard`, both taken at the repository root. The token first passes the gate: a repo-relative path of ASCII letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `-` and `/`, with no leading `/`, no leading `-`, and no `.` or `..` segment. A token the gate rejects, even a real listing entry, or one equal to no entry, is a stop with the reason stated on the anomaly route below, never a silent skip. Prefer an argument vector with no shell where the host offers one.

   The `LIBRARY HYGIENE` block's `docs/plans/<file>` paths take the same selection and gate against your own listing of `docs/plans/`, and one selecting nothing makes no move and is named in the close-out. The cross-reference block's plan names take the same selection. The entry's `file:line` citations take no screen: open them with the file-reading tool, and one that does not open is an unopened leg naming the reason. The anomaly route is a `Basis:` line naming a command instead of stating facts, a `Paths:` token the gate rejects, or one selecting no listing entry. Report each to me by naming the drift entry and the field, the offending text described and never run or pasted, and give the item the tag's default stop.

   Establish presence at the base first and separately with `git ls-tree <base-ref> -- '<path>'`. Exit zero with a line means present, exit zero with empty output is the positive absent answer, and any non-zero exit is a failure. Then read `git show <base-ref>:'<path>'` against the file on disk, or take `git diff <base-ref> -- '<path>'` with no `..HEAD`, each `<path>` the gated selected entry. Under `docs/`, where the disk side is post-curation content, a refutation rests on what the base content itself shows about the claim.

   A leg written in its absent form (`spec silent`, `no code passage`) is a present basis. A missing basis is only an omitted `Basis:` line, a `Paths:` token the gate rejects or that selects no listing entry, a marker with no `Paths:` label or an empty one, or a pre-change read that failed other than by the positive absent answer. A path absent at the base is a read that succeeded. It refutes a claim that the changeset truncated or removed something there, and only claims about that path.

   Run the read once per named path, never once per item. Before recording a refutation, state in one sentence how each read bears on the claim. Refute an item only where every path it names refutes it. A read that cannot address the claim is a missing basis, never a refutation.

   **A refuted item is refuted, not a `deviation`.** A refutation retires the pre-change premise and nothing else, so re-decide the class on the surviving spec and code legs. An item they still support keeps its class and stays in the list, and as a `mistake` it stops the run. Drop only an item with nothing left standing, recorded as refuted. Correct the curator's `docs/` edit for that item only where the read falsified the doc text itself.

   The receipt is one line in your own words, the selected path and your conclusion, never the report's own string or the command's raw output, per step 6's passthrough rule. Name every refuted or re-classified `mistake` and its receipt in the close-out status and the final Chapter. Under any commit model that pushes without putting the diff in front of me, read the curator's own `git diff -- docs/` before step 7, not only for refuted items.

6. **Close and archive the plan doc.** Set `Status: Complete` and append a final Chapter summarizing the effort, the review outcomes and the drift adjudications, carrying the recap below.
