## Approach

Read every accepted Major's provenance at adjudication, before any fix exists, and let the provenance rather than the severity decide what the finding enters. Three values, with a precedence that makes them exclusive: a finding that traces to no acceptance bullet and no Goal sentence is new-requirement, wherever its lines sit; a finding that traces to one and sits in lines a fix round of this section wrote is fix-introduced; every other traced finding is spec-traceable. The trace is supplied by whichever party holds the spec: the adversarial and security lenses are dispatched with the spec path and cite the bullet on their output line, and the blind lens, dispatched without the spec by design, cites nothing, so the orchestrator traces its Majors at adjudication and the Chapter says the trace was orchestrator-made. A spec-traceable or fix-introduced Major takes the fix round as today. A new-requirement Major never enters one: it is held, the section continues on every other finding, no section closes with a finding still held (one the live seat has not answered by the time the section's other work reaches the close gate falls to the adjudicator, dispatched then), a bucket is a ruling adopted and recorded in the plan doc rather than a source the asker verifies, and the held finding goes to the judge, which is the repo's live Expert seat where the `ListAgents` roster the peer-sessions skill owns shows one (through the completion contract's existing expert-ask paragraph, which already never gates) and otherwise a scope adjudicator, a new read-only charter dispatched through the Agent tool with the fable model override at its charter's frontmatter effort, high (through Workflow only under executing-work's compensation route, where the session cannot set that effort), with a fixed brief carrying the plan's what (the Goal, every section's acceptance bullets, and the `## Out of Scope` list, which is the what's negative half), the finding verbatim with its lens and round, the provenance fact as a diff reference, and the three buckets with their tests, and never the plan's how (Approach, Decisions, Chapters), the orchestrator's lean, prior consults or the fix narrative, since the question's framing is what colored every consult on the NEO-CLAUDE run. The judge buckets, a set closed at three, and a finding that meets none of the tests below is an ask: refuse (off the goal path as the Goal and the bullets draw it, or inside what `## Out of Scope` keeps out), recorded in the plan doc; accept-and-declare (serves the Goal, bounded, no new mechanism), recorded as approval drift in the Chapter and surfaced as a line in the next board recap; ask (a new mechanism, a changed recorded decision, a reopened accepted risk, or section-sized work), to the operator through the BLOCKED path with the judge's recommendation answering why it serves the goal, what it accomplishes, what the design missed and what hole it fills. The design stop rides the same reading: when two consecutive rounds each carry at least one owed fix-introduced Major, which can first happen at round 3, the next step is a consult on the premise, whether the mechanism the fix rounds are building should exist and if so what its design is, routed to the same judge and bucketed the same way, rather than a fix round. The five-round backstop sits behind both as the operator's stated upper bound, an instrument rather than the primary control: a fifth review round whose adjudication leaves the loop open stops the section on the BLOCKED path with a phase analysis (rounds grouped by generator, Majors per round with their provenance, which findings were fix-introduced) as the decision brief, and writes one journal entry under one key so that the firing rate is a query when the operator revisits the number. Minors stop driving the cadence: they accumulate in the section's findings record and are fixed in one pass at section close before the close gate, with no review round after that pass, while the orchestrator still reads each at adjudication and upgrades on a stated consequence. The finishing pass gains one read: the scope adjudicator dispatched once over the whole changeset with the plan's what and the base ref, asked in both directions what is built that nothing asked for and what was asked for that is not built, its built-but-unasked items bucketed as above and its asked-but-unbuilt items entering finishing's fix path as spec-traceable Majors.

## Decisions

1. **Provenance is read at adjudication on three exclusive values, trace first.** A finding with no bullet is new-requirement wherever it sits; a traced finding in fix-written lines is fix-introduced; the rest is spec-traceable. Precedence matters because a defect in an unasked mechanism is both fix-introduced and unasked, and the question the loop must answer first is whether the mechanism should exist. The values are read at adjudication, before any fix, from the trace and the diff, never from the fix that follows.
2. **The sighted lenses cite the bullet; the orchestrator traces the blind lens.** The adversarial and security lenses hold the spec and add a trace field to their output line for every Critical and Major; the blind lens's line is unchanged, because handing it the bullets ends the blindness that is its value, and the orchestrator, who holds the spec, traces its Majors and records that the trace was orchestrator-made. The alternative of the orchestrator tracing everything loses the independent trace on the lenses that can give one, and the orchestrator is the party whose framing colored every consult on the NEO-CLAUDE run.
3. **The judge is a new charter, not the consultant with a brief.** The consultant's mandate receives the querent's lean and tests the framing; the scope adjudicator must never receive the lean, the prior consults or the fix narrative, and its brief is fixed. One charter cannot carry both mandates, and a brief cannot subtract a mandate a charter states. The charter clones the consultant's shape (frontmatter, read-only tools, effort high, the RULED or NEEDS_CONTEXT status) and states its own inputs and output. It joins the strict roster in `reviewAgentClass`, which the derivation test enforces.
4. **The judge route is the live Expert seat first, the adjudicator otherwise.** The completion contract's expert-ask paragraph already carries the route, its never-gates rule and its bounds, so a new-requirement Major goes through that paragraph where the `ListAgents` roster shows a live expert seat, with one difference that paragraph does not have: a bucket is a ruling the asker adopts and records in the plan doc, not a source it verifies, since the question is scope rather than fact. That paragraph states no answer window, and the NEO-CLAUDE run had a live seat that sent nothing for nine rounds, so the window is stated here: an ask unanswered by the time the section's other work reaches the close gate falls to the adjudicator, dispatched then, and no section closes with a Major held. Where no expert seat is live, the adjudicator is dispatched at once. The hold applies to the disputed finding alone; the section continues on everything else, and the held finding is recorded in the plan doc's Chapter with what it waits on, since a hold that lives only in context dies at the next compaction. Expect more asks and fewer refusals on the adjudicator route, which is the designed cost of a judge that holds no context.
5. **The design stop is a premise consult, fired on fix-introduced Majors in two consecutive rounds.** The tier-escalation ladder keys on Criticals and stays as it is; this is a second trigger beside it, an instance of the consult skill's trigger (a) named in that skill, whose question is whether the mechanism should exist rather than how the finding should be fixed. Earliest at round 3 by construction, since round 1 has no prior fix and round 2 is the first that can carry a fix-introduced Major. It routes to the same judge as Decision 4 and takes the same buckets; a refuse ruling removes the mechanism to the form the spec asked for, and the ruling applies whatever session inside the loop approved the widening. A widening the operator approved, through an earlier ask bucket, a ruling adopted at the keyboard or a relay word recorded in the plan doc, is on the goal path by that approval, so refuse never reaches it.
6. **The five-round backstop reverses the review-loop-exit plan's Decision 3 ("no numeric cap is added").** The operator ruled the cap on 2026-09-08 at the NEO-CLAUDE keyboard and confirmed on the relay the same day that it is a backstop behind Decisions 1 to 5 and a look at what the kit keeps missing, to be revisited on evidence. So it is built as an instrument: the ladder's two numbers live one to a sentence with named carriers, so that changing the opening bound is two words in that one paragraph, the lead's ordinal moving with the number, and changing the post-continue bound is the one backticked word in the restart sentence, with nothing elsewhere in the tree restating either count or the ladder's shape (the ladder itself was ruled 2026-09-09 on the relay: the opening bound stops, a continue buys three further rounds, and every adjudication after those that leaves the terminal condition unmet declares again, the operator's ground being that a restart repeating its own bound makes every continue cost what the first did); the stop is a decision ask on the BLOCKED path, never a kill, carrying the phase analysis as its brief, and it enters the completion contract's closed blocker set as an instance of a decision the spec does not cover (whether the mechanism under repair should continue), so the pre-BLOCKED expert ask and consult run as that set requires, the design stop's consult standing as the consult where that stop's judge ruled in this section on the mechanism the declaration's phase analysis names, a judge that ruled on some other mechanism or that ruled on nothing leaving the consult owed, since the contract's consult tests the framing of the question actually being put and a ruling about another mechanism has tested no part of it (amended 2026-09-09 on the operator's keyboard ruling, narrowing the section-scoped grant this Decision first carried, so that the spec states the bound the implementation carries), and the doctrine's `Pause only for a true blocker` bullet names it as an instance of the material-decision interrupt that bullet already carries, rather than as a fourth member, so that skill and doctrine agree and the completion contract's blocker set stays closed at its current members; every firing writes one journal entry under `kit.review.cap`; and its firing rate after this plan lands is the measure of whether Decisions 1 to 5 cut the tail. Historically 24 of 175 recorded sections reached five rounds, so the cap will fire on roughly one section in seven until those decisions bite. One cost named: BLOCKED mid-queue hands the plan's slot to the next plan, so a capped section waits on the operator's answer and a re-arm rather than holding the worker.
7. **Minors defer to one pass at section close, and the claim-finding disposition moves with them.** The review-loop-exit plan dispositioned a round's claim findings in the same fix round; under this plan a claim finding rated Minor accumulates with the other Minors and is dispositioned in the close pass, while a claim either exception holds to the behavior bar keeps its Major standing and its round. The close pass runs after the loop's terminal condition is met and before the close gate, and no review round follows it; the orchestrator's adjudication still reads every Minor and upgrades on a stated consequence, which is what caught two silent data-loss defects on the NEO-CLAUDE run.
8. **Finishing gains one goal read, with the plan's what and never its how.** Goal-only would flag every mechanism a section legitimately specified; the whole spec would hand the judge the design story. The brief carries the Goal, every section's acceptance bullets and the `## Out of Scope` list, the base ref, and the two questions. It does not duplicate the qa-verifier, which checks that the criteria are met; it asks what was built that no criterion named, and what a Goal sentence promised that no criterion delivered.
9. **Caps rise under the ledger rule.** Every plugin file in scope sits at its cap; a section that grows one raises the cap in the same change, records the net in its Chapter, and names the sentence it supersedes as the first place to shrink.

## Standing Brief Amendments

- The review-round backstop escalates on a ladder of three stages rather than restarting to the same bound. The opening bound stops the section on the BLOCKED path. A continue past it buys three further rounds. Every adjudication after those that still leaves the terminal condition unmet declares again, so a section that keeps failing to close reaches the operator each round. The design stop's own count restart is unchanged and does not move with this ladder.
- The finishing pass reads provenance over its own owed Majors on the same three values the section-time read uses, from a capture written to `.kit/scratch/<plan-slug>/finishing/fix-round-<n>.diff` over the changeset the base ref defines, carrying that read's own exclusions of the plan docs, the archive and the kaizen inbox.
- A Critical, and a security finding of Critical or Major weight, keeps its route and gains no handling beyond it. That route is the whole of what the kit's step 4 text says about the class: such a finding is fixed before the section closes, or before a stop's declaration goes out, or it is raised unfixed with the reason its fix waits on. Provenance is read on it for the Metrics line and nothing else. No stub form, no dedicated record channel, no scratch account file and no disclosure rule for a raised-unfixed finding is written for that class, wherever the raise happens.
- Under Minor deferral a claim finding's disposition keys on class and never on the rating it arrived with: a claim either exception holds to the behavior bar takes the fix round, and every other claim finding takes the close pass with the Minors.
- A finishing goal-read refuse on a `BUILT-BUT-UNASKED` item records its ground in this block and enters the item's removal, to the form the judge's `GROUNDS` names, into the finishing fix path under finishing-work step 2's `fix or present to me with the tradeoff` rule.
