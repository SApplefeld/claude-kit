## Approach

A skill nobody loads shapes no behavior, so its text is weight with no reader. Retirement is a deletion plus what its references need. Two kinds of reference exist and the plan treats them apart. A pointer names the skill as a place to go and leaves the rule whole when removed: the doctrine's "the `cold` skill is the dedicated tool for those verdict moments" is one, and so is the ownership map's row naming `recap`. A rule reference is a sentence whose meaning is the skill's content: the coordinator skill declares its drain window and says the steps a drained session takes "are that skill's to state", executing-work's `WAITING:` shape names "a park at a safe boundary per the park skill" as one of its two occasions and says "the park skill's drain runs first", kit-goal's leash condition (c) names the same occasion, and peer-sessions says a leashed receiver "parks on a drain line by the park skill's own steps". Strip those and five surviving rules govern the parking moment with no owner, which the doctrine's one-owner-per-moment bullet calls a defect. So the park retirement waits on the operator saying where the drain steps live once the skill is gone, and the plan carries that question with a recommendation rather than deciding it.

Where a pointer cannot be removed without changing a rule's meaning, the implementer stops and names the sentence in the Chapter rather than rewriting the rule. A test whose subject leaves the tree retires with it, per the testing-discipline skill, and that is a strip under this plan; a test that counts surfaces the deletion changes is corrected to the new count together with every prose figure that states the same count.
