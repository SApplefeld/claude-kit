# Kaizen inbox: SCOTT-DESKTOP

- 2026-08-02 | SCOTT-DESKTOP | sapplefeld-claude-kit | `executing-work`'s tier-escalation rule only knows how to escalate the model. When review rounds keep returning Criticals because the spec's premise is wrong rather than the implementation, the rule points at a stronger implementer, which cannot help: each round faithfully builds a brief that is itself mistaken. The missing discriminator is whether the findings repeat or keep moving. Repeating findings mean the implementer is missing something and the tier is the lever; findings that keep moving to new ground mean the spec is wrong and the escalation is to the operator with a decision brief, not up the model ladder. Add that fork to the escalation rule so a session does not spend a tier bump on a moving target.
