# Kaizen brief: bracket read-only-intent dispatches the guard does not govern

Friction: a read-only *band* is not a read-only *guard*. The readonly-agent-guard binds a fixed roster (the strict reviewer class plus the qa-verifier, `plugins/claude-kit/hooks/readonly-agent-guard.js` `agentClass`); a scout dispatched as Explore or general-purpose carries Bash the guard never reads, so a brief's read-only instruction is a request, not a control, and the tree-state bracket that would catch a mutation after the fact is scoped to review rounds only (executing-work step 3). Scouts fire constantly mid-section with unstaged work in the tree, bracketless. General lesson: a rule stated as a property of the output ("the tree stays untouched") loses to the convenient path; the durable control is mechanical (a guard) or observational (a bracket), never the brief's wording.
